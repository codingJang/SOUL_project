## Political Data

### Intergovernmental Organizations (IGO) Dataset

**Citation:**
Pevehouse, Jon C.W., Timothy Nordstron, Roseanne W McManus, Anne Spencer Jamison, "Tracking Organizations in the World: The Correlates of War IGO Version 3.0 datasets," *Journal of Peace Research*.

**Original Dataset Citation:**
Wallace, Michael, and J. David Singer. 1970. "International Governmental Organization in the Global System, 1815-1964." *International Organization* 24: 239-87.

**Description:**
The Intergovernmental Organizations (IGO) dataset tracks the status and membership of intergovernmental organizations from 1815-2014. IGOs are international organizations that have at least 3 nation-states as their members. The dataset enables analysis of multilateral cooperation patterns, institutional membership networks, and the evolution of international governance structures over two centuries.

**Dataset Details:**
- Period: 1815-2014
- Coverage: All intergovernmental organizations with 3+ nation-state members
- Data Collection: 5-year intervals from 1815-1965, annually thereafter
- Three formats: IGO-year, country-year, and dyad-year units of analysis

**Links:**
- Dataset Homepage: https://correlatesofwar.org/data-sets/igos/
- Hosted by: Timothy Nordstrom (University of Mississippi), Jon Pevehouse (University of Wisconsin), and Megan Shannon (Colorado-Boulder) under the COW Data Hosting Program

### Defense Cooperation Agreement Dataset (DCAD)

**Citation:**
Kinne, Brandon J. 2020. "The Defense Cooperation Agreement Dataset (DCAD)," *The Journal of Conflict Resolution* 64(4): 729-755.

**Description:**
The Defense Cooperation Agreement Dataset (DCAD) is a comprehensive, human-coded dataset of bilateral defense cooperation agreements (DCAs) covering all independent countries for the period 1980-2010. DCAs are formal international agreements that coordinate and institutionalize the routine, day-to-day defense relations of their signatories, including joint military exercises, peacekeeping operations, defense research, weapons programs, officer exchanges, and policy coordination.

**Dataset Details:**
- Period: 1980-2010
- Agreements: 1,872 unique bilateral defense cooperation agreements
- Coverage: All independent countries
- Sources: UN Treaty Series, World Treaty Index, country sources, global newspaper archives

**Links:**
- Dataset Homepage: https://www.brandonkinne.com/dcad
- Access via Correlates of War: https://correlatesofwar.org/

**Note:** This analysis filters DCAD data to agreements from 2000 onwards to focus on contemporary defense cooperation patterns.
